SECTION 3

Signal Fusion Architecture & Disparity Veto

At 1:N scale across an entire city or district gallery, soft biometrics alone yield unacceptably high false-match rates. Soft signals must act as conditional confirmations or hard geometric pruning gates rather than independent identity verifiers.

🚫 Hard Geometric Pruning Gates

• Height Disparity Veto: Δ > 12.0cm triggers immediate geometric veto.
• Proportion Disparity Veto: Torso/leg ratio Δ > 0.30 pruned before ranking.
• Kinematic Stride Veto: Stride delta > 25.0cm suppressed.

⚖️ Conditional Confirmation Rule

• Soft biometrics (gait, height, body, clothing) act as conditional confirmations only.
• Face/FRS required for automatic high confidence (≥ 78%).
• Unverified soft biometrics capped at 45% and routed for mandatory officer review.
